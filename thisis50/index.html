The villa

Le Haut des Espinets

A hilltop Provençal villa 2.5 km from the medieval village of Saint-Paul-de-Vence, looking out over the Mediterranean to one side and the foothills of the Alps to the other. It was built in 1982 by the architect Alain Berp, who reclaimed the ceiling beams from an old Portuguese monastery. The Burgundy stone floors still show their fossils.

Getting there

Fly into Nice. We'll take it from there.

Nice Côte d'Azur airport (NCE) is about 20 minutes from the villa. Each family books its own travel. The villa opens at 4pm on Saturday 12 June, so aim to land by mid-afternoon, or arrive the night before and stay in Nice. We leave by 10am on Saturday 19 June, so book a late-morning or afternoon flight home.

From San FranciscoSFO → London or Paris → Nice
Best routes: British Airways nonstop to London Heathrow, then British Airways on to Nice. Or Air France nonstop to Paris Charles de Gaulle, then Air France on to Nice. Either way it's one change on one airline, so bags go straight through. Allow about 15 hours.

Best route: Sacramento has no flights to Europe, so come down to the Bay Area and fly with the Vantress Family from SFO, via London or Paris as above. We can travel together.

Why Nice, not Paris or Lyon?

Nice is 20 minutes from the villa. Lyon is about four and a half hours' drive, and Paris is another flight or a six-hour train. Fly into Paris only if you'd like a few days there first, then hop down to Nice. Book by January: June is peak season on the Riviera. Check that every passport is valid for at least three months after 19 June and was issued within the last ten years. The EU is introducing a short online travel authorisation, called ETIAS, for UK and US visitors; check the official ETIAS site a few weeks before you fly. The villa strongly recommends travel insurance.
